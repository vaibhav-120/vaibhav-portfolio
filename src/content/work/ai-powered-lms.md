## The problem

Recorded lectures don’t answer back. Learners need to ask questions while they’re confused, and instructors need to know who actually understood the material.

## What I built

An interactive learning experience with live lectures, where learners talk to the AI instructor by voice in real time over WebSockets.

Live assessment workflows evaluate learners’ answers and produce structured performance reports, so understanding and progress can be tracked over time.
